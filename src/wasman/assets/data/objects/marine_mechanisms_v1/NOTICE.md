WASMAN modifications: authored industrial appearance, materials and decorative
hardware, generated by scripts/prepare_panel_mechanisms.py. Original mechanism
collision geometry and physical USD attributes are preserved and checked by the
builder. Scene/controller changes are separately versioned and documented.
These assets are not manufacturer CAD or calibrated hardware models.

The grounded panel references the existing ship_green panel; its CC0 Poly Haven
texture provenance is in ../../panels/ship_green/provenance.json. Its lower wall
extension has collision and a continuous PBR visual shell.
